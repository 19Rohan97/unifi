Before you ask

Quick answers

How fast will I hear back?

Within one business day — usually faster. Form submissions and voicemails land with a real person, not a queue. If your matter is urgent, call (313) 899-5420 during business hours.

Do you charge for quotes or site surveys?

No. Scoping calls and standard site surveys are free. You get a line-item proposal with exact hardware, labor, and timeline — no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

All of Michigan. We're based in Taylor and work daily across Metro Detroit, Ann Arbor, Lansing, Grand Rapids, Flint, and beyond. For the right project we travel statewide.

Do you handle residential projects?

Our focus is commercial and multi-tenant work — offices, warehouses, schools, medical, retail. We take on select residential projects (typically larger homes needing structured cabling, WiFi, or camera systems). Ask — worst case we point you to someone good.
